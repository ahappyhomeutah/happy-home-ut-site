# A Happy Home Website

Build a slick, easy-to-use, friendly and inviting marketing website for a Utah DSPD residential group home provider called “A Happy Home.” The goal is to encourage guardians, support coordinators, families, and referral sources to contact the company. Use the uploaded logo image from this chat as the brand logo if available, and match the primary palette to the logo: soft powder or periwinkle blue background and bright white typography. Keep the look clean, warm, modern, trustworthy, professional, and disability-services appropriate.

Brand and audience:
- Company name: A Happy Home
- Industry: DSPD group home and residential habilitation support provider in Utah
- Instagram handle: ahappyhome.ut
- Audience: guardians, parents, support coordinators, case managers, DSPD and DHHS partners, and potential employees
- Tone: warm, safe, clean, friendly, professional, home-like, respectful, person-centered
- Avoid: sterile hospital look, overly corporate design, generic disability imagery, cluttered government feel

Design requirements:
- Primary colors: soft blue from logo and white. Use subtle darker blue or blue-gray text for readability and small accents.
- Use rounded cards, soft shadows, generous spacing, large friendly headings, and simple icons.
- The logo should appear in the header and footer.
- Header should be sticky or prominent, with navigation: Home, Services, Why A Happy Home, About, Careers, Contact.
- Primary CTA buttons: “Contact Us,” “Make a Referral,” and “Call Now.”
- Mobile-first, fast, accessible, and easy to scan.
- Include clear contrast and readable font sizes.
- Use friendly home-style visual blocks rather than heavy clinical imagery.

Website structure:
1. Home / Hero
- Large hero section using brand blue background and white/blue text.
- Include logo mark and headline: “A safe, happy home for adults with disabilities.”
- Subheadline: “Person-centered residential support in Utah, built around dignity, comfort, daily routines, community access, and real relationships.”
- CTAs: “Contact Us” and “Make a Referral.”
- Add small trust badges/phrases: “DSPD Residential Support,” “Utah-Based Provider,” “Person-Centered Care.”

2. Services section
- Residential Habilitation Support / group home support
- Daily living support and routines
- Medication assistance and health appointment coordination
- Community access and skill-building
- Personal budget / representative payee coordination wording should be careful: “support with budgeting and coordination when authorized”
- Family/guardian communication
- Use concise cards with icons.

3. Why A Happy Home
- Emphasize warm home environment, consistent routines, individualized plans, respectful staff, clean and comfortable homes, communication with guardians and teams, safety and compliance.
- Include a section titled “Care that feels personal, not institutional.”

4. About section
- Describe A Happy Home as a Utah provider focused on helping individuals feel safe, respected, and included.
- Mention working alongside guardians, support coordinators, families, medical providers, and DSPD teams.
- Use: “We follow applicable Utah DSPD/DHHS requirements and person-centered planning expectations.”

5. Referral/contact section
- A high-converting contact form with fields: Name, Role, Phone, Email, Who are you contacting us about?, Services interested in, Message.
- Contact CTA copy: “Have a question or referral? We would love to talk.”
- Add buttons for phone and email placeholders: Phone: “Call A Happy Home”; Email: “Email Us.” Use placeholder hrefs or editable values.

6. Careers mini-section
- Friendly section inviting caring staff to apply.
- CTA: “Ask About Open Positions.”

7. Instagram / social section
- Include a clean Instagram link button to Instagram handle ahappyhome.ut.
- Display simple social cards or a “Follow life at A Happy Home” section; do not require Instagram API.

8. Footer
- Logo, quick links, contact CTA, service area: Utah, and a short disclaimer: “Information on this website is general and not a guarantee of eligibility, placement, or services. Services depend on individual needs, authorizations, and applicable program requirements.”

Functionality:
- Contact form should be present and user-friendly. It can save/display submission state locally or be ready to connect to email/form backend later.
- Smooth scrolling nav.
- Responsive layout for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- SEO basics: title, meta description, semantic headings.
- Build as a polished modern website, not a bare template.

Content style:
- Plain English, warm, short paragraphs.
- Make the website feel inviting to families while still credible to support coordinators.
- Use the company colors throughout; do not introduce many unrelated colors.
- Overall feel: clean, friendly, professional, with brand blue and white leading the visual identity.
